## Remaining acceptance boundaries

- No outgoing call, SMS, payment, or real order was initiated. Receiving-phone handling and payment settlement need a real operator acceptance check.
- Delivery origin, mileage basis, and route fee schedule are unspecified. Eligibility and fee are confirmed manually; no automatic distance calculation or geofence is implemented.
- Physical iPhone Safari, Add to Home Screen, offline/PWA behavior, AirDrop/phone-touch sharing, and payment-app recipient privacy were not device-tested. This report does not certify those capabilities.
- No historical customer submissions were accessed or changed. This was functional website UAT with basic headers/asset checks, not a penetration test or a complete WCAG audit.
